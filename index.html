INSIDE THE WORKSPACE

From authorized access to useful evidence.

The application runs on the operator’s device. This website explains the service; it does not collect logins or show private analytics.

01

Connect deliberately

The account owner grants the required read permissions through the platform’s official authorization screen. Optional permissions are requested only when needed.

02

Keep each observation

Public video details and available metrics are saved as dated snapshots in a local database. Missing metrics remain unavailable, and earlier results are not fabricated.

03

Review in context

Reports compare videos by platform and observation age. Experiment notes remain separate from claims of proven causation.

THE APPROACH

Useful insight starts with clear boundaries.

↗

Official access

Account owners authorize access through each platform’s official OAuth flow and APIs.

◷

Honest timelines

New observations are saved with timestamps. Earlier results are never reconstructed as if they had been measured.

◎

Read-only analysis

This analytics integration does not publish, edit, or delete social media content or manage comments.
