Nomad Memories Social Lab

Terms of Service.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Nomad Memories Social Lab is a local analytics application for reviewing authorized social media account and video performance data. These terms apply to use of its read-only analytics integration.

1. Authorized use

You may connect only an account that you own or are authorized to manage. You are responsible for following the applicable terms and policies of YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok when authorizing access.

2. What the integration does

The integration uses official platform APIs to read account information, public video metadata, and available performance metrics. It stores timestamped observations locally to support reports and comparisons. Available data and permissions vary by platform and account.

The read-only analytics integration does not publish, edit, or delete videos or manage comments.

3. Account access

Connecting an account requires an official OAuth authorization flow. You can withdraw platform access through the relevant platform’s connected-app settings. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not by itself erase previously stored local observations. See the Privacy Policy for deletion details.

4. Availability and accuracy

Platform APIs, permissions, rate limits, and returned metrics can change. Reports reflect the data actually available at the time of each observation. Missing data is identified as unavailable rather than estimated. Analytics are informational and do not guarantee audience growth or other outcomes.

5. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated when the integration or platform requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page.
